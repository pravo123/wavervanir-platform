Why now

SR 26-2 pulls AI and agentic models into model-risk scope.

SR 26-2 took effect on 17 April 2026 and supersedes SR 11-7. The update makes explicit that machine-learning, generative, and agentic systems are models in the supervisory sense, so they fall under the same expectations for validation, documentation, and oversight as any traditional risk model. If you deploy AI in decisions, you now own the model-risk burden.

Sound model risk management still rests on three pillars. Every AI system you field has to answer to all three.

1

Conceptual soundness

Is the model built on defensible theory and data, with assumptions and limitations written down and challenged?

2

Ongoing monitoring

Once in production, is performance tracked against benchmarks, with drift, data quality, and override behavior watched continuously?

3

Outcomes analysis

Do realized results get compared back to what the model predicted, with the findings feeding the next review cycle?
